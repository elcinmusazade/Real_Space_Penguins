# Ares: First Light cinematic trailer

- Produced a standalone 30-second trailer from the published ten-Sol game. Captured the construction-kit selection, ice discovery at A3, solar array at D3 and the Sol 02 energy forecast. Animated the captured game artwork for close-ups.
- Mixed a synthesized Mission Control voice with the game’s interface tones and an excerpt of Stefan Grossmann’s *Peaceful Village* (CC BY 4.0). English captions are burned into the video. The end card credits the artist and the MP4 metadata includes the source and license links.
- Exported `ares-first-light-trailer.mp4` in the workspace’s `outputs` folder. Verified the complete decode, 900 frames at 1920×1080 and 30 fps, 30.000 seconds of picture and 48 kHz stereo AAC, and the legibility of the captions, gameplay readouts and credits.
